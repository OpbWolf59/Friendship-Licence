About Friendship Licenses 💭

What is a Friendship License?

A Friendship License is an official certification of your bond with your best friend. It serves as a beautiful digital record of your friendship, complete with memories, preferences, and a personalized license card. It's a fun and heartfelt way to celebrate the special connections that matter most.

Features

  • ✓ Official Friendship License Card with unique ID
  • ✓ Digital Memory Gallery to store precious moments
  • ✓ Shared Likings to celebrate common interests
  • ✓ Affidavit and Certificate of Friendship
  • ✓ Full customization and personalization

Getting Started

1. Visit the "License" page to view the friendship certificate
2. Upload memories in the "Gallery"
3. Manage your shared likings and preferences
4. Share and celebrate your friendship!
